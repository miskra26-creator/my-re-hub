Paper-Trading Bot

Runs a strategy and auto-places simulated orders on Alpaca. Fake money only.

Backtester Live Bot
Paper trading = fake money. Not financial advice. This bot can place orders on its own. Always test with Dry run first. It only ever talks to Alpaca's paper endpoint, so no real money is at risk — but a bot that trades poorly with fake money would trade poorly with real money too. Prove an edge here before you ever think about real funds.

One-time setup

  1. Create a free account at alpaca.markets and open the Paper Trading dashboard.
  2. Generate API keys (Key ID + Secret) for paper trading.
  3. Create a file named .env inside the trading-platform folder with: 
    APCA_API_KEY_ID=your_key_id
APCA_API_SECRET_KEY=your_secret_key
  4. Restart the app (npm start). This page will then show your paper account.
